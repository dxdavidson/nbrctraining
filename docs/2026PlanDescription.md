# NBRC 2026 / 2027 Ergo Training Plan

The 2026 / 2027 training plan is an evolution of what we have been doing over recent years. It adopts some of the principles of the Wolvering Row Training Plan, but has been adapted to suit us 
- We are not college athletes, but are considerably older, therefore 3 sessions a week is the target (not 9 as younger athletes may do)
