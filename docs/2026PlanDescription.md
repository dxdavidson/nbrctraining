The 2026 / 2027 training plan is an evolution of what we have been doing over recent years. It is based on the Wolverine Row Training Plan, created by Mike Caviston, but has been adapted to suit us 
- We are not college athletes, but are considerably older, therefore 3 sessions a week is the target (not 9 as younger athletes may do)
- We normally schedule 1 hour sessions, which limits workouts to 45 minutes, to allow for warmup.
- I have retained the 2k test for Block 3, even though a skiff 2k takes 12 minutes, because 2k is the standard benchmark, its what training plans use, and it allows us to set goals for ourselves where we can compare our performance to the rowing community at large. 

## Keys to Success with This Training Plan
- For L4 workouts stick to the stroke rate stated, the pace guidance is secondary
- For L2 & L3 workouts, stick to the pace target, the stroke rate is secondary
- Use the low rates to improve your technique, not to get your best splits 
- Train with the intensity specified for the workout and use the pace guidance
- Warm up appropriate to the intensity of the workout, L4 minimal warmup, L1 maximum warmup (at least 8 minutes of a stuctured warmup).
- Consistency, aim for 3 workouts every week


## Workout Intensities (L1-L4)
Workouts are labelled with a planned intensity (L1-L4), this will often come with a pace guidance, based on the intensity of the workout, and your current 2K estimate (if you don't have an estimate of your 2K then best thing is to do a 2K and find out). 

### L1 - Maximal Effort
- Purpose: Measure performance; sharpen top‑end speed
- Intensity: All‑out
- Examples: 2k test, 5k test, 1‑min max, 500m max

### L2 - High‑Rate Power / VO2
- Purpose: Develop race‑pace power, high‑rate control, and oxygen uptake
- Pace **95%** of your 2K estimate, hitting this pace is primary goal (stroke rate is purely a rough guide)
- Intensity: Very hard but repeatable
- Stroke Rate: R26–32 (depending on block)
- Examples: 4 × 4 min, 6 × 3 min, 10 × 1 min

### L3 - Threshold / Hard Steady
- Purpose: Build sustainable race‑pace endurance
- Pace **80%** of your 2K estimate, hitting this pace is primary goal (stroke rate is purely a rough guide)
- Intensity: Controlled discomfort; “comfortably hard”
- Stroke Rate: R20–24
- Examples: 3 × 10 min, 2 × 12 min, 20 min continuous

### L4 - Aerobic Base / Rate‑Restricted
- Purpose: Aerobic development, technique, efficiency
- Stick to the stroke rates, this is primary goal (pace is a rough guide)
- Intensity: Steady, sustainable
- Stroke Rate: Strictly R18–20
- Examples: 30–40 min continuous, countdowns, long intervals

## Blocks
The plan will include 3 blocks

### Block 1 - Oct-Dec
- Benchmark: 30R20 (row 30 minutes at 20 spm)
- Aerobic base & technique
- L4 primary focus
- L3 and L2 used to start to develop 
### Block 2 - Jan - Mar
- Benchmark: 5K
- Threshold development
- L3 primary focus

### Block 3 - Apr - Race Season
- Benchmark: 2K
- Race power & speed
- L2 primary focus
