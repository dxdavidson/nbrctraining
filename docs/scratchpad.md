## Key Objectives 
Build endurance and improve technique.  

Use the L4 workouts on this block to hone your technique, hit the stroke rates and if you're erging in company, its more important to get into the same rhythm than it is to focus on pace.   

## Warmup 
- For workouts starting at L4 workouts 
   - See [Pick Drill](../training/technique/rowing-machine) videos. 
   - Pick Dril: from finish position
      - Arms Only x10
      - Arms + Body x10
      - Half Slide x10
      - Full Slide x10
    - Reverse Pick Drill: from catch
       - Legs Only x10
       - Legs + Body x10
       - Legs + Body + Arms (full stroke) x10
-  For L1 workouts go through a full 8' minute warmup (available under Other Workouts plan)
